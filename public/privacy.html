Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 29, 2026

This policy describes the current intended product behavior. The site owner should obtain qualified legal review for the jurisdictions and business entity that actually apply before accepting new paid customers.

What we collect

When you use Plant Doctor, we process the photo you choose, the plant name you provide or confirm, requested language, generated guidance, IP-derived usage key, and technical request information. If you subscribe or create a membership, we process your email, language, membership status, payment provider, campaign attribution, and delivery status. Payment-card details are handled by Stripe or PayPal and are not intended to be stored by Garden with Serge. Support requests may contain information you choose to provide.

How information is used

We use information to provide plant-photo guidance, enforce usage limits, activate memberships, deliver requested email, process support/cancellation/refund requests, secure and troubleshoot the service, and measure aggregate service performance. We do not promise that AI output is correct.

Service providers

Plant photos and prompts may be sent to OpenAI to generate guidance. Payments may be processed by Stripe or PayPal. Email may be delivered through Brevo. Hosting, database, and analytics providers process the minimum information needed for their functions. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply.

Retention and security

We aim to retain personal data only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal obligations, security, dispute resolution, and backup cycles. Exact production retention periods and deletion-from-backup procedures must be finalized and documented before paid launch. We use access controls and transport encryption, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe through the link in marketing email. You may request access, correction, or deletion through the contact page. Some billing and transaction records may need to be retained where legally required. Do not upload a photo containing people, addresses, documents, or other personal information.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses a member cookie for access, local browser storage for language and usage display, and may use Google Analytics. Analytics must not receive plant photos, email addresses, diagnosis text, or payment data. Consent behavior may vary by applicable law and will be implemented before promotion in affected regions.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian should supervise use by minors.

Operator and contact

Garden with Serge is operated by Serge Shammas, 20249 Livorno Way, Los Angeles, CA 91326, USA. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to contact@gardenwithserge.com or submitted through Contact Garden with Serge.

Changes

Material changes will update the date above.
